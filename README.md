# Pentoniq
PENTONIQ Tube Synth for macOS • by John Roland Penner
October 28, 2026, Toronto

PENTONIQ Tube Synth (Release Candidate RC1) for macOS 🎹 growls and purrs with pure Tube Harmonic TONE. deep resonant filters with nuanced control of the harmonics; an ADSR Envelope that connects to the Filter to make changes in performance affect the Brilliance of the generated tone. 🌶️ put some hotsauce in your mix! a lot of plugins let you splash some digital after-sauce on to a lifeless oscillator — PENTONIQ bakes the goods right into the signal generation. live natural open sounds that use classic tube modelling and Analogue Oscillator design to give you the best sounds in your musical productions. 

Two Full Poly-Tube Oscillator Voices • VCO Oscillator • VCF Resonant Filters • Classic Controls • Resonant MIX Control + Oscilloscope 🎶 Sub-OSC, MIDI LINK, Preset Tone Selector, Arpeggiator, Digital Delay, Leslie speaker simulation. 

TESTED on: macOS 27 golden gate + LOGIC 12.3 on M1 MacMini 16Gb RAM
